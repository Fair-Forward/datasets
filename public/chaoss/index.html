Community Health (CHAOSS metrics)

Each project's panel shows CHAOSS metrics for its repositories, models and records on GitHub, Hugging Face and Zenodo, measured from public data. The definitions come from CHAOSS, the Linux Foundation's Community Health Analytics in Open Source Software project, which does not review or endorse this catalogue.

Rules

  • "Last 12 months" means the 365 days up to the day of measurement.
  • Response time needs at least 5 outside issues and pull requests, pull request closure at least 5 outside pull requests. Outside: anyone but the owner, organisation members and collaborators.
  • Bots are left out of people counts, response times, pull request closure and the last change on GitHub (Bot Activity).
  • Measured every Monday at 09:00 UTC. If a source cannot be read, a project can keep its previous measurement for up to 28 days.

Coverage

37 of 89 projects link measured assets in their dataset or use-case links: 32 GitHub repositories, 35 Hugging Face models, datasets or spaces and 5 Zenodo records. 11 more projects link only to pages not measured yet: Kaggle, Harvard Dataverse, figshare, GitLab and organisation pages on Hugging Face or GitHub. Not counted: private or gated content, non-default branches, work outside repositories.

Use in the catalogue

The default order adds up to 40 points for recent changes, stars and downloads to a documentation score of up to 100; a project whose links all failed at the last check loses 20 points. Insights compares SDGs, not projects, as CHAOSS advises, and shows figures only for an SDG with at least 5 measured projects.

Privacy and corrections

Only counts and dates are published, never contributors' names, usernames or email addresses; private repositories are skipped. To correct a value or leave a project out, open an issue or write to jonas.nothnagel@gmail.com.

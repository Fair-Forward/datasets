Privacy

What this site measures

We count visits so we can tell whether the catalog is reaching the people who could reuse what is in it. We do not use cookies, we do not build profiles, and nothing here identifies you.

Visit statistics

This site uses Umami, an open-source analytics tool, hosted in the European Union. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so there is no consent banner to click.

For each page view it records:

RecordedWhy
Page visitedWhich datasets and use cases people actually open
Referring siteWhether a link we shared brought anyone here
CountryWhether the catalog reaches the regions it is built for
Browser, operating system, device typeWhether the site works on the devices people use

Umami states that it collects no personally identifiable information and anonymises what it does collect. Your IP address is used to work out a country and is not part of the statistics we can see. We cannot identify you, follow you across other websites, or connect a visit to any other visit. See Umami's privacy policy for their side of it.

Other services this site loads

Being straight about this: a few things on the page are fetched from other companies' servers, and any such request necessarily reveals your IP address to them.

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site, so GitHub (Microsoft, USA) handles every request.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface.
  • Cloudflare serves the icon set.

None of these are used by us for tracking, and we set no advertising or third-party tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share visit data with anyone.

Data you send us deliberately

If you open an issue on GitHub or email us, we keep what you send in order to reply. Nothing on this site asks you to log in or fill in a form.

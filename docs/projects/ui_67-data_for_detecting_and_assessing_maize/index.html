Data for Detecting and Assessing Maize Stress

Country: South Africa | SDG 2, SDG 11 | Data: Geospatial/Remote Sensing, Images, Drone Imagery | Maturity: Dataset

Assesses the capabilities of Earth observation and machine learning algorithms, specifically Random Forest and Support Vector Machines, in detecting maize disease stress using drone data. The study was conducted on a small-scale farm in Limpopo, South Africa, focusing on healthy maize, maize with streak virus, and maize with phosphorus deficiency.

Data characteristics

Low-res images, (hyper)spectral variability for phosphorus deficiency & MSV.

Model characteristics

Random Forest, SVM; <80% accuracy; Software/Application: Drone + ML (RF, SVM)

How to use

Requires high-quality imagery, better spectral resolution; GEE and drone access recommended

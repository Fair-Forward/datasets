Agro-thermography

Country: South Africa | SDG 2, SDG 11, SDG 13 | Data: Geospatial/Remote Sensing, Images, Tabular | Maturity: Dataset

Explores the integration of ground-based vegetation parameters, thermal infrared data from handheld cameras, and UAV multispectral data to map crop canopy temperature at high resolution. The research, conducted at the Ha-Mphaila irrigation scheme in Limpopo, South Africa, focused on tomato crops.

Data characteristics

Thermal IR and multispectral image fusion and field data + Drone Data

Model characteristics

Regression model; canopy temperature estimation; Software/Application: Thermal camera + UAV + regression models

How to use

Needs UAV & thermal cam; costly setup unless shared or borrowed

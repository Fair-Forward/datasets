Enhancing business registration in Kenya through a chatbot

Country: Kenya | SDG 8 | Data: Text | Maturity: Dataset > Model > Pilot > Use case

The BRS-chatbot is an AI-powered chatbot that streamlines the business registration process in Kenya. It aimes to enhance access to information, simplify the registration process, improve user experience through Human-Centered Design principles, and promote compliance with post-registration requirements. The goal is to reduce reliance on intermediaries and make the registration process more user-friendly and efficient
